## Ingredients

- 500g Canned Lentils
- 2 Small Yellow Onions
- 8 Cloves Garlic, pressed
- 2 Tbsp Tomato Paste
- 1/2 Cup Parsley
- 1/2 Cup Mint
- 1 Tbsp Cumin
- 1 Tbsp Paprika
- Pitas
- 1 Tsp Ground Cinnamon
- 1 Tsp Black Pepper
- 1 Tsp Allspice
- Salt
- Nooch
- 4 Tbsp Flour
- 1/2 Cup Greek Yoghurt
- 3 Tbsp Tahini
- Lemon Juice
- 1 Tbsp Olive Oil

## Steps

0. (clarify if the 5.3 is correct via recipe card)
1. Rinse the lentils, put in a bowl, and mash until ~50% are mashed
2. Shred the onion and squeeze out all the liquid
3. Add in tomato paste, 5.3 Tbsp parsley and mint, cumin, paprika, cinnamon, pepper, allspice, salt (to taste), nooch, flour, and olive oil
4. Stuff the pitas and cook. Open side first, then other sides
5. Blend together the greek yoghurt, tahini, remaining parsley and mint, 1 tsp cumin and paprika, lemon juice
