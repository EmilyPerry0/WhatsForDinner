## Ingredients

- 1 Head of Garlic
- 3 Red Bell Peppers
- 10 oz Carrots
- 1/2 Head Cauliflower
- 3 Tomatoes, quartered
- 1 Yellow Onion, cut into chunks
- 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 2 Tsp Salt
- 2 Tsp Paprika
- 1 Tsp Cayenne Powder
- 1 Tsp Cumin
- 2 Cubes Vegetable Bouillon
- 1 Lemon

## Steps

1. Cut top off garlic, cut carrots into bits
2. Cut other veggies as usual
3. Place onto a pan, and cover with oil and spices
4. Bake at 200°C for ~ 40 minutes
5. Meanwhile, heat up water and bouillon on stove
6. Blend veggies with bouillon until soupy consistency is achieved
7. Serve with grilled cheese
