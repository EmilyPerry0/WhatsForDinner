## Ingredients

- 1 Box Oyster Mushrooms
- 2 Tsp Cumin
- 2 Tsp Paprika
- 2 Tsp Oregano
- 2 Tsp Basil
- 1 Tbsp Tomato Paste
- 1 Lemon
- Olive Oil
- 1 Tsp Garlic Powder
- 1 Tsp Onion Powder
- Salt
- Pepper
- Sauce
- Tortillas
- Toppings

## Steps

1. Coat mushrooms in marinade mix (all ingredients)
2. Bake mushrooms at 200°C until brown, ~25 minutes
3. Chop up and place in wrap with desired sauce and toppings
