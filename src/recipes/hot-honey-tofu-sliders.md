## Ingredients

- 1 Cup Oat Milk
- 1/2 lemon's juice
- 2 Tsp Smoked Paprika
- 2 Tsp Garlic Powder
- 2 Tsp Onion Powder
- 2 Tsp Pepper
- Salt
- 2 Cups Flour
- 1 Tbsp Vegetable Bouillon
- 2 Cups Crushed Corn Flakes
- Slider Rolls
- Siracha Sauce
- Honey
- Cayenne Pepper
- Ranch
- Pickles


## Steps

1. Mix oat milk, lemon juice, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp pepper, salt, 1 1/2 cups flour, bouillon
2. In a separate bowl, mix corn flakes, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic pwder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp pepper, salt, 1/2 cup flour
3. Mix equal parts honey and siracha in a separate bowl
4. Mix ranch with a dash of salt and lots of cayenne
5. Dip tofu cutlets in milk mixture, then in cornflakes and fry
6. Coat in honey mixture and serve on bun with pickles and ranch
