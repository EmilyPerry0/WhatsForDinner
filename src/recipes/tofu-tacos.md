## Ingredients (Tofu)

- 400g firm tofu
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
-  Tbsp oil
- 2 Tsp cumin
- 1 Tsp Paprika
- salt

## Ingredients (Sauce)

- 1 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 Tsp cumin
- 1 Tsp Paprika
- 1/2 Tsp chili powder
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1/3 cup (80ml) pineapple juice
- 1/2 Tbsp soy sauce

## Ingredients (Salsa Verde)

- a bunch cilantro or parsley
- 2 cloves garlic
- 4 Tbsp vegan yogurt
- 4 Tbsp vegan mayo
- 1/2 lemon
- 1 Tsp cumin
- 1 Tsp agave
- salt to taste

## Ingredients (Other)
- Tortillas (medium)
- Tomato

## Steps

1. Pat tofu dry and shred
2. Toss tofu in the tofu ingredients and spread on a baking sheet
3. Bake for 30 minutes at 175°C, stirring every once in a while
4. Fry onion and garlic in a pan with some oil until softened
5. Add in cumin, paprika, chili powder, and tomato paste and roast for 2-3 minutes
6. Stir in pineapple juice and soy sauce and bring to a bubble. then reduce heat and stir in the baked tofu.
7. Blend salsa verde ingredients until smooth
8. serve the tofu in tortillas with fresh veggies and the salsa
