## Ingredients

- 300g Cherry Tomatoes
- 3 Tbsp Olive Oil
- 1/2 Tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
- 4 Cloves Garlic
- 4 Sprigs Thyme
- Salt
- Black Pepper
- 200g Feta
- 100g Cream Cheese
- 1 Tsp Honey
- Bread

## Steps

1. Slice tomatoes in half
2. Mix vinegar, 2 Tbsp oil, garlic, thyme, pinch of salt, and pepper with tomatoes. Bake for 15 minutes at 180°C
3. Blend together feta, cream cheese, 2 tbsp olive oil, honey, pinch of salt, and pepper
4. Top cheese mix with baked tomatoes, serve with toasted bread
