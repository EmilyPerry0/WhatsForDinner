## Ingredients

- 200g Tempeh
- Shredded White Cabbage
- Bean Sprouts
- 3 Spring Onions
- 3 Cloves Garlic
- 1 Red Chili
- 3 Tbsp Ketjap Manis
- 1 Tbsp Dark Soy Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Oyster Sauce
- 1 Tsp Samal Oelek
- 2 Packets Instant Ramen Noodles

## Steps

1. Cook Tempeh: Heat oil and add tempeh with a pinch of salt, cook for ~ 7 minutes
2. Add 1 tbsp of kepjap manis, cook until caramelized
3. Add in cabbage, bean sprouts, spring onions, garlic, and red chili
4. Cook until cabbage had wilted
5. Add in noodles, remaining ketjap manis, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, and samal oelek
6. Toss until combined. lekker smaken
