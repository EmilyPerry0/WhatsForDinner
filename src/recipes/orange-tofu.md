## Ingredients (Tofu)

- 1 Block Tofu
- 1 Tbsp Soy Sauce
- 1 Tsp Onion Powder
- 2 Tbsp Cornstarch

## Ingredients (Sauce)

- 1/2 Cup Orange Juice
- 1 Tbsp Soy Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 3 Tbsp Maple Syrup
- 1 Tsp Seasame Oil
- 3 Cloves Garlic (pressed)
- 1/2 Cup Vegetable Broth
- 1 Tsp Red Pepper Flakes
- Salt
- 1 Tsp Siracha
- 1 Tbsp Cornstarch
- Green Onion
- Broccoli

## Steps

1. Cube Tofu and coat in tofu ingredients
2. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes
3. 5 minutes before tofu is ready, mix tgoether sauce ingredients and begin to simmer
4. When tofu is done, add tofu to the sauce pan and put broccoli in oven
5. When sauce is desired consistency, serve over rice with broccoli and green onions
