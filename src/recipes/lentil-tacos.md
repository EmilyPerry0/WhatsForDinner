## Ingredients

- 250g Cooked Lentils
- 1 Tbsp Harissa Paste
- 150g Halved Cherry Tomatoes
- 100g Feta
- Rocket Leaves
- 4 Small Tortillas
- Hummus
- 100g Plain Yoghurt
- 1 Lemon
- Small Handful Chopped Mint
- Salt
- Pepper

## Steps

1. In a bowl mix lentils, harissa paste, cherry tomatoes, and crumbled feta
2. In another bowl mix yoghurt, lemon juice, chopped mint, salt, and pepper
3. Spread hummus onto each wrap, spoon lentil mixture onto half and rocket onto other half
4. Fold over and fry in some oil on stovetop
5. Dip in yoghurt mixture
