## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp coconut oil (or any neutral oil)
- 1 Tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 Tsp mustard seeds
- 1 inch fresh ginger (finely grated or minced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 green chilli sliced (optional, adjust to taste)
- 1 medium white onion diced (about 150g)
- 2 medium tomatoes diced (about 200g)
- 190g red lentils rinsed and drained
- 600ml vegetable stock
- 400ml full-fat coconut milk
- 1 Tsp ground turmeric
- 2 Tsp garam masala
- Juice of 1/2 lemon (plus more to taste)
- 1 Tsp salt (or more to taste)
- 3 Tbsp fresh coriander cilantro leaves, chopped

## Steps

1. Heat the coconut oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and mustard seeds. Let them sizzle and pop for about 30–45 seconds until fragrant.
2. Add the ginger, garlic, green chilli (if using), and diced onion. Sauté for 4–5 minutes until the onion softens and becomes translucent.
3. Add the rinsed red lentils, diced tomatoes, vegetable stock, coconut milk, turmeric, garam masala, and salt. Stir everything together well.
4. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low. Cover with a lid and simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are completely soft and creamy. (Red lentils cook quickly and will break down – that’s what you want!)
5. Remove the lid, taste, and adjust seasoning – add more salt or a good squeeze of lemon juice if needed.
6. Stir in the fresh coriander leaves (and a handful of baby spinach if you’d like some greens).
7. Serve hot with rice, naan, or flatbread. Top with extra coriander, chilli flakes, and lemon wedges.
