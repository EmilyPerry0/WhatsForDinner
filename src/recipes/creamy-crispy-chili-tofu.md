## Ingredients

- 400g Tofu
- 2 Tsp Curry Powder
- 2 Tbsp Cornstarch
- 2 Tbsp Soy Sauce
- 4 Tbsp Skyr
- 4 Tbsp Siracha Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Mayo
- 1 Tbsp crispy chili oil
- 2 Tsp Salt
- 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- 50 ml Soy Milk

## Steps

1. Cut tofu into small chunks and boil in salted water
2. Mix. curry powder, cornstarch, and soy sauce together and then marinate tofu in mix
3. Bake at 180°C for ~25 minutes
4. Mix together skyr, mayo siracha, chili oil, salt, garlic powder, soy milk
5. Serve over rice
