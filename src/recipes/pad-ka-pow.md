## Ingredients

- 4 Jalapenos
- 4 Habaneros
- 2 Anaheims or Serrenos
- 1/2 Cup Chopped Green Beans
- 1/2 Small Onion Diced
- 300g Tofu
- 1 Tbsp Oyster Sauce
- 1 Tbsp Soy Sauce
- 2 Tbsp Fish Sauce
- 1 Tsp Worcestershire Sauce
- 2 Tbsp Water
- 2 Tsp Sugar
- 1 1/2 Cup Basil Leaves
- 1 Egg pp (optional)

## Steps

1. Pound spicy peppers to fine paste
2. Pound garlic andmild peppers to rough paste
3. Combine sauces, water, and sugar. stir to dissolve the sugar
4. In a wok, sautee the garlic chili paste until garlic is golden brown
5. Add tofu and toss until no longer in big clumps
6. Add sauce and toss until tofu is almost done
7. Add onions and green beans, finish cooking tofu
8. Remove from heat and add basil
