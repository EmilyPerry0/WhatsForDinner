## Ingredients

- 4 15oz Cans of White Beans
- 1 Cup Cream Cheese
- 1 1/3 Cup Sour Cream
- 6 Tbsp Guac/Ranch Seasoning
- 1 1/2 Cup Buffalo Sauce
- 1/2 Cup Water
- Salt
- 2 Cups Shredded Cheddar
- 8 Sprigs Green Onion

## Steps

1. Blend: 2 cans beans, cream cheese, sour cream, ranch seaoning, buffalo sauce, salt (to taste)
2. Pour mixture into baking pan, add remining beans, half of the cheddar, half of the spring onions and mix
3. Top with remaining cheddar and bake for 30 mins at 200°C
4. Sprinkle remaining spring onions on top and serve.
