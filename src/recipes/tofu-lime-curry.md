## Ingredients

- 1 block tofu, pad dry & cut into cubes
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 red onion, diced
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 small ginger piece, minced
- 1 red chili, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp paprika powder
- 1 tsp mustard powder
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 can full fat coconut milk
- 1 lime

## Ingredients (Toppings)
- cilantro
- Pickled onions
- Red chili, sliced
- Lime wedges
- Rice


## Steps

1. Coat the tofu cubes in the cornstarch, olive oil & salt. Bake in the airfryer (or oven) at 200C for 20 minutes.
2. Meanwhile sauté the red onion in 2 tbsp of oil on medium heat for 4-5 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, ginger & chili and sauté for 30 seconds.
4. Add the tomato paste & spices. Toast for 30 seconds.
5. Add the bell pepper, coconut milk and mix well.
6. Add the tofu cubes, frozen peas & simmer for 5 minutes.
7. Serve and garnish with the toppings
8. ENJOY!
