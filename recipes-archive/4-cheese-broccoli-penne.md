## Ingredients

- 16 oz Penne
- 10 oz Chopped Broccoli
- 1/2 Cup Butter
- 1/2 Cup Flour
- 4 Cups Milk
- 2 Tsp Salt
- 2 Tsp Pepper
- 4 oz Shredded Mozzarella
- 4 oz Shredded Sharp Cheddar
- 4 oz Shredded Swiss
- 4 oz Shredded Parmesean

## Steps

1. Preheat to 400°F
2. Cook pasta to al-dente
3. In a medium saucepan on medium heat, melt butter. Once melted, whisk in butter, making a roux. Whisk in milk.
4. Add granulated garlic, mozzarella, cheddar, and swiss.
5. Fold in broccoli and place in dish, top with parmesean
6. Bake for 20 minutes
